# Equipment Guide

## Kitchen Equipment

### Rice Cooker (1.8L)
Perfect for meal prep and multi-tasking:
- **Hainanese chicken + rice together** - Cook protein and carbs simultaneously
- **Steam vegetables** using steamer basket while rice cooks
- **Hard-boil eggs** on top while cooking rice
- **One-pot pasta meals** for quick dinners

### Air Fryer
Ideal for batch cooking and reheating:
- **Batch cook seasoned chicken thighs** - Portuguese, cajun, or tandoori
- **Reheat pre-cooked proteins** without drying them out
- **Quick vegetable cooking** - frozen vegetables in 8-10 minutes
- **Temperature:** 200°C for 25-30 minutes for chicken

### Stove
For daily fresh cooking:
- **Daily egg scrambling** - 2 minutes for breakfast
- **Pasta cooking** - fresh pasta when needed
- **Quick stir-fries** - combine pre-cooked proteins with fresh vegetables

## Gym Equipment Focus

### Cable Machines
Versatile and beginner-friendly:
- **Lat pulldown** - Upper back strength for climbing
- **Seated cable row** - Posture and pulling power
- **Cable face pulls** - Shoulder health and tennis serve
- **Cable wood chops** - Rotational power for golf and tennis

### Machine-Based Training
Safe progression with controlled movements:
- **Leg press** - Lower body power without balance demands
- **Chest/shoulder press** - Upper body strength development
- **Leg extension/curl** - Isolated muscle targeting
- **Hip abductor/adductor** - Lateral stability for court sports
