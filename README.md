# Balanced 4-Week Home Workout Plan

## Week 1-2: Foundation Building (12-18 min sessions)

### Monday - Core & Grip
- 40 sec [plank](#plank)
- 12 [dead hangs](#dead-hangs) on beastmaker jugs (5-6 sec each)
- 25 [modified crunches](#modified-crunches) (or [dead bugs](#dead-bugs) if preferred)
- 12 resistance band [pull-aparts](#band-pull-aparts)
- 40 sec [side plank](#side-plank) (each side)
- 3 x 10 sec focused [jug hangs](#jug-hangs)

### Wednesday - Shoulders & Back
- 3 x 12 resistance band [face pulls](#band-face-pulls)
- 12 band [overhead presses](#band-overhead-press)
- 15 band [rows](#band-rows) (controlled tempo)
- 12 band [lateral raises](#band-lateral-raises)
- 12 band [reverse flies](#band-reverse-flies)
- 2 x 15 sec [jug hangs](#jug-hangs)

### Friday - Power Circuit (2 rounds)
- 15 [burpees](#burpees) or [mountain climbers](#mountain-climbers)
- 8 [assisted pull-ups](#assisted-pull-ups) on jugs (use feet as needed)
- 15 resistance band [woodchoppers](#band-woodchoppers) (each side)
- 12 band [bent-over rows](#band-bent-over-rows)
- 40 sec [hollow body hold](#hollow-body-hold)
- Max time [jug hang](#jug-hangs) (aim for 20+ sec)

### Optional Sunday - Active Recovery
- 5 min gentle band stretching
- 10 easy hangs (5 sec each)
- Light core work (choose 3 favorites)

## Week 3-4: Progressive Overload

### Progression Options:
- Add 10 sec to all planks/holds
- Add 2-3 reps per exercise
- Add 1 more round to Friday circuit
- Decrease rest between exercises

### Advanced Variations to Try:
- [Plank](#plank) → Plank with leg lifts
- Regular [hangs](#dead-hangs) → Offset hangs (different height holds)
- Band [rows](#band-rows) → Single arm band rows
- [Assisted pull-ups](#assisted-pull-ups) → Negative pull-ups

## Quick Start Hacks:
1. **2-Minute Rule**: Just do the first exercise - momentum usually kicks in
2. **Workout Clothes = Uniform**: Put them on as soon as you're home
3. **"Just Hang" Rule**: If you really can't start, just do one jug hang
4. **Music Trigger**: Same pump-up song = workout time
5. **Check-off Calendar**: Visual progress is powerful

## Form Cues:
- **Planks**: Shoulders over wrists, glutes tight
- **Hangs**: Shoulders engaged (not shrugged up)
- **Band Work**: Control the return, don't let it snap back
- **Core**: Quality over speed - feel each rep

## Equipment Setup:
- Keep resistance band draped over beastmaker (visual cue)
- Clear 6x6 ft space for floor work
- Have water bottle ready

Remember: Consistency > Perfection. Three 15-minute workouts beat one missed 45-minute session!

# Exercise Explanations

## Core Exercises

### Plank
- Start in push-up position, lower to forearms
- Body straight from head to heels
- Engage core, don't let hips sag
- Breathe normally throughout

### Side Plank
- Lie on side, prop up on elbow
- Stack feet, lift hips off ground
- Keep body in straight line
- Hold, then switch sides

### Modified Crunches
- Lie on back, knees bent
- Feet flat on floor
- Arms crossed over chest (easier) or hands beside ears (harder)
- Curl up just until shoulder blades lift off ground
- Focus on using core, not neck muscles

### Dead Bugs
- Lie on back, press lower back into floor
- Start with arms extended toward ceiling and knees bent at 90°
- Lower one arm overhead while extending opposite leg
- Keep back pressed into floor throughout
- Return to start position and alternate sides

### Hollow Body Hold
- Lie on back, press lower back down
- Lift shoulders and legs off ground
- Arms extended overhead
- Hold position, maintain lower back contact

### Band Woodchoppers
- Anchor band high or low
- Rotate across body diagonally
- Use core, not just arms
- Control both directions

## Progression for Core Exercises
- **Modified Crunches → Bicycle Crunches**: Once you can do 30 modified crunches with good form, try bicycle crunches
- **Dead Bug → Bird Dog**: When dead bugs feel comfortable, try bird dogs for more challenge
- **Short Hollow Hold → Longer Hold → Hollow Rocks**: Start with 10-second holds, progress to 30+, then add rocking

## Back & Pull Exercises

### Dead Hangs
- Hang from jugs with arms straight
- Shoulders slightly engaged
- Relax everything else
- Time each hang

### Jug Hangs
- Same as dead hangs but on beastmaker jugs
- Focus on grip endurance
- Keep shoulders from shrugging up
- Breathe steadily

### Assisted Pull-ups
- Start hanging from jugs
- Use feet on chair/ground for support
- Pull up until chin over hands
- Lower with control

### Band Pull-Aparts
- Hold band at shoulder height
- Arms straight in front
- Pull band apart to chest
- Squeeze shoulder blades together

### Band Face Pulls
- Anchor band at face height
- Pull handles to face
- Elbows high, squeeze shoulder blades
- Control the return

### Band Rows
- Anchor band at chest height
- Pull handles to ribs
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Slow release forward

### Band Reverse Flies
- Bend forward slightly, band anchored
- Arms out to sides at shoulder height
- Squeeze shoulder blades
- Maintain slight elbow bend

### Band Bent-Over Rows
- Hinge at hips, band under feet
- Pull handles to lower ribs
- Keep back straight
- Squeeze at top

## Shoulder Exercises

### Band Overhead Press
- Stand on band, handles at shoulders
- Press overhead fully
- Keep core tight
- Lower with control

### Band Lateral Raises
- Stand on band, arms at sides
- Raise arms to shoulder height
- Lead with elbows
- Control the descent

## Dynamic Exercises

### Burpees
- Squat, place hands on floor
- Jump feet back to plank
- Optional push-up
- Jump feet forward, stand up

### Mountain Climbers
- Start in plank position
- Alternate bringing knees to chest
- Keep hips level
- Move quickly but controlled
