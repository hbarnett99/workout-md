## Weekly Schedule Overview
- **Monday**: Gym Session 1 (30 min) - Upper Body Focus

- **Wednesday**: Office day - 15 min home workout (optional)

## Important Health Notes

### Managing Nausea/Lightheadedness

**Pre-Workout (2-3 hours before)**
- Eat a light meal with carbs and protein
- Avoid heavy, fatty, or high-fiber foods

**30-60 minutes before gym**
- Have a small snack (banana, handful of nuts, or toast)
- Drink 500-600ml of water

**During workout**
- Sip water between exercises (don't chug)
- Take 60-90 second rests between sets
- If feeling unwell, stop immediately and sit down

**Intensity Guidelines**
- Start with lighter weights/easier variations
- Rate of Perceived Exertion: Stay at 6-7/10 (moderate)
- You should be able to hold a conversation during exercises
- Gradually increase intensity over 4-6 weeks

**Red Flags - Stop Exercising If You Experience:**
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Nausea
- Chest pain or unusual shortness of breath
- Extreme fatigue

## Progression & Weight Guidelines

### General Principles
- **Week 1**: Start with lighter weights to learn form
- **Week 2-3**: Increase by 10-20% if form is good
- **Week 4+**: Increase when you can complete all sets with 2+ reps in reserve

### Weight Progression Schedule
**Upper Body Machines:**
- Lat pulldown: Start 35-45kg → Progress to 50-60kg → Target 65-75kg
- Chest press: Start 25-35kg → Progress to 40-50kg → Target 55-65kg
- Shoulder press: Start 15-25kg → Progress to 30-40kg → Target 45-55kg

**Lower Body Machines:**
- Leg press: Start 60-80kg → Progress to 100-120kg → Target 140-160kg
- Leg extension: Start 20-30kg → Progress to 35-45kg → Target 50-60kg
- Leg curls: Start 15-25kg → Progress to 30-40kg → Target 45-55kg

**Core/Functional Machines:**
- Rotary torso: Start 10-20kg → Progress to 25-35kg → Target 40-50kg
- Cable exercises: Start 10-20kg → Progress to 25-35kg → Target 40-50kg

### Progression Plan
- **Week 1-2**: Focus on form, use lighter weights
- **Week 3-4**: Increase weight/reps by 10-15%
- **Week 5-6**: Add intensity or complexity
- **Week 7+**: Reassess and adjust based on tennis performance and how you feel

## Flexibility Benefits for Sports

### Tennis
- **Shoulder flexibility** improves serve power and reduces injury risk
- **Hip mobility** enhances court movement and reach
- **Spinal rotation** increases racquet head speed and shot accuracy
- **Hamstring flexibility** helps with low shots and quick direction changes

### Golf
- **Thoracic spine rotation** is crucial for a full shoulder turn
- **Hip flexibility** allows proper weight transfer and follow-through
- **Shoulder mobility** prevents restrictions in the backswing
- **Ankle mobility** improves stability and power transfer

### Climbing
- **Hip flexibility** essential for high steps and heel hooks
- **Shoulder mobility** prevents impingement on overhangs
- **Hamstring flexibility** helps with mantling and high feet
- **Spinal mobility** improves body positioning on varied terrain

## Recovery Tips
- Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep
- Stay hydrated throughout the day
- Consider a post-workout protein snack
- Listen to your body - rest when needed
- **Hold stretches for 30+ seconds** for flexibility gains
- **Breathe deeply during stretches** - never hold your breath
- **Stretch consistently** - flexibility improves gradually over weeks/months

## Exercise Technique Guide

### Upper Body Exercises

#### Lat Pulldown Machine
Sit with thighs under pads, grab bar wider than shoulders. Pull bar to upper chest, squeeze shoulder blades. Control the weight back up. Lean back slightly for better angle.

#### Seated Cable Row
Sit upright, feet on footrests, pull handle to lower chest. Squeeze shoulder blades together at the end. Keep torso stable throughout. Focus on pulling with your back, not just arms.

#### Shoulder External Rotation Machine
Set cable at elbow height, stand sideways. Keep elbow at 90°, rotate arm away from machine. Control the return. Keep upper arm stationary throughout the movement.

#### Cable Crunch
Kneel facing cable machine, hold rope attachment at head. Crunch down by flexing spine, pause, control back up. Keep hips still. Focus on using abs, not pulling with arms.

#### Cable Face Pulls
Stand facing cable machine at upper chest height. Pull handles to face, elbows high, squeeze shoulder blades. Great for posture and rear delts. Control the return.

#### Chest Press Machine
Sit with back flat against pad, grip handles at chest level. Press forward smoothly until arms are extended, control back to start. Keep shoulders back and core engaged.

#### Shoulder Press Machine
Sit with back supported, grip handles at shoulder height. Press up overhead until arms are extended, lower with control. Keep core tight throughout.

#### Tricep Dip Machine
Sit in dip machine or stand at cable. Press down/push body up by extending arms. Focus on tricep contraction. Control the negative portion.

### Lower Body Exercises

#### Leg Press Machine
Sit in machine, feet shoulder-width apart on footplate. Lower weight by bending knees to 90°, push back up through heels. Keep core engaged and back pressed against pad.

#### Leg Extension Machine
Sit with back against pad, ankles behind roller pads. Extend legs smoothly to straight position, squeeze quads at top. Lower slowly with control. Don't swing or use momentum.

#### Leg Curl Machine
Lie face down or sit (depending on machine), ankles under/behind pads. Curl heels toward glutes, squeeze hamstrings at top. Lower slowly with control. Keep hips pressed down.

#### Seated Calf Raise Machine
Sit with balls of feet on footplate, weight pads on thighs. Rise up on toes as high as possible, pause briefly, lower slowly. Feel the stretch at bottom.

#### Hip Abductor/Adductor Machine
Sit in machine, adjust pads to outside/inside of thighs. Push legs apart (abduction) or squeeze together (adduction). Control the movement both ways. Keep torso upright.

### Core & Rotational Exercises

#### Rotary Torso Machine
Sit with back against pad, hold handles. Rotate torso to one side against resistance, return to center. Complete all reps one direction before switching. Keep movement controlled.

#### Cable Wood Chops
Stand sideways to cable machine, hold handle with both hands. Pull from high position diagonally down across body. Keep core engaged, rotate with control. Step can be included for more power.

## Flexibility & Stretching Guide

### Upper Body Stretches

#### Doorway Chest Stretch
Stand in doorway, place forearm on frame at shoulder height. Step forward gently until you feel stretch across chest and front of shoulder. Keep shoulder down and back. Great for opening chest after desk work.

#### Cross-body Shoulder Stretch
Bring one arm across your body at chest height. Use opposite hand to gently pull arm closer to chest. Keep shoulders square and relaxed. Feel stretch in back of shoulder and upper back.

#### Overhead Tricep Stretch
Raise one arm overhead, bend elbow so hand reaches down your back. Use other hand to gently push elbow back. Keep torso upright and don't force the stretch. Feel it along back of arm.

#### Neck Side Bends
Slowly tip head to one side, bringing ear toward shoulder. Don't lift shoulder to meet ear. Hold gentle stretch along side of neck. Return to center before switching sides.

#### Upper Trap Stretch
Tilt head to one side while reaching opposite arm down and back. You can sit on your hand to increase the stretch. Feel it along the side of neck and top of shoulder.

### Lower Body Stretches

#### Standing Quad Stretch
Stand on one leg, bend other knee and grab ankle behind you. Keep knees together and push hips forward slightly. Use wall or chair for balance if needed. Feel stretch along front of thigh.

#### Standing Figure-4 Hip Stretch
Place ankle of one leg on opposite knee (like sitting cross-legged but standing). Sit back slightly and lean forward gently. Use wall for balance. Feel stretch in hip and glute of elevated leg.

#### Calf Stretch Against Wall
Place hands on wall, step one foot back. Keep back leg straight, heel on ground, lean into wall. Feel stretch in calf of back leg. Switch to bent knee version for deeper calf muscle.

#### Standing Hamstring Stretch
Place one heel on low surface (step, curb, bench). Keep leg straight, lean forward from hips. Keep back straight, don't round shoulders. Feel stretch along back of elevated leg.

#### Standing Spinal Twist
Stand with feet hip-width apart, place hands on hips or cross arms. Rotate torso to one side, keeping hips facing forward. Hold and breathe, then return to center before switching.

### Full Body & Recovery Stretches

#### Cat-Cow Stretches
Start on hands and knees. Arch your back and look up (cow), then round your spine and tuck chin to chest (cat). Move slowly and focus on spinal mobility. Great for morning stiffness.

#### Hip Flexor Stretch (Kneeling Lunge)
Kneel in lunge position, front foot flat on ground. Keep torso upright and push hips forward gently. Feel stretch along front of hip of back leg. Don't lean forward or arch back excessively.

#### Seated Forward Fold
Sit with legs extended, reach forward toward feet. Keep spine long, hinge from hips rather than rounding shoulders. Don't force it - just breathe and let gravity help. Feel stretch in hamstrings and lower back.

#### Supine Spinal Twist
Lie on back, bring knees to chest, then drop them to one side. Keep shoulders on ground, extend arms in T-shape. Breathe deeply and let gravity help the twist. Switch sides slowly.

#### Seated Spinal Twist
Sit cross-legged or with legs extended. Place one hand behind you, other hand on opposite knee. Gently rotate spine, looking over shoulder. Keep spine tall, don't collapse. Great for mid-back mobility.

#### Downward Dog to Upward Dog Flow
Start in downward dog (inverted V), shift forward to plank, lower to low push-up, then press up to upward dog (chest up, arms straight). Flow smoothly between positions, stretching entire spine.

#### Child's Pose with Side Reach
Kneel and sit back on heels, fold forward with arms extended. Walk hands to one side to stretch along ribs and back. Hold, then switch sides. Very relaxing and great for lower back.

#### Pigeon Pose
From hands and knees, bring one knee forward toward same-side wrist. Extend back leg straight behind you. Lower down onto forearms if comfortable. Deep hip opener - go slowly and never force it.

#### Seated Figure-4 Stretch
Sit in chair, place ankle on opposite knee. Gently lean forward while pressing down on raised knee. Feel stretch in hip and glute. More accessible version of pigeon pose.

#### Gentle Backbend
Lie on back with knees bent, lift hips into bridge pose. Or stand and place hands on lower back, gently arch backward. Counteracts forward posture from daily activities.

#### Shoulder Wall Slides
Stand with back against wall, arms in goal-post position (elbows and wrists touching wall). Slide arms up and down wall while maintaining contact. Great for shoulder mobility and posture.

### Recovery & Breathing

#### Deep Breathing with Overhead Reach
Sit or stand tall, inhale while raising arms overhead. Exhale while lowering arms. Focus on expanding ribcage and deepening breath. Helps activate parasympathetic nervous system.

#### Deep Breathing with Gentle Movement
Combine gentle movements (arm circles, gentle twists) with deep breathing. Move slowly and focus on coordination of breath and movement. Helps transition from exercise to rest.

#### Gentle Sun Salutation Flow
Modified yoga sequence: Mountain pose → forward fold → half lift → forward fold → mountain pose. Move slowly with breath. Can add downward dog and low lunge for full sequence.

#### Final Relaxation
Lie comfortably on back (legs up wall) or sit in child's pose. Close eyes, breathe naturally, let body completely relax. Scan from head to toe, releasing any tension. End each session mindfully.

### Wrist & Forearm Care

#### Wrist Circles & Flexor Stretches
**Wrist circles:** Extend arms, make slow circles with wrists in both directions. Start small, gradually increase size.
**Flexor stretch:** Extend arm forward, palm up. Use other hand to gently pull fingers back toward you. Feel stretch along forearm. Essential for tennis players to prevent overuse injuries.

#### Wrist Extensor Stretch
Extend arm forward, palm down. Use other hand to gently push hand down and back toward you. Feel stretch along top of forearm. Balances the flexor stretch - both sides need attention.

#### Prayer Stretch
Place palms together in prayer position at chest. Lower hands while keeping palms together until you feel stretch in wrists and forearms. Hold gently. Great for desk workers and racquet sports.

#### Tendon Glides
Make a fist, then straighten fingers, then make "claw" shape, then back to fist. Move smoothly through positions. Helps tendons glide properly and prevents stiffness from repetitive gripping.

### Tennis-Specific Preparation

#### Tennis-Specific Arm Swings
**Cross-body swings:** Swing one arm across body and back, mimicking backhand motion.
**Overhead swings:** Swing arm up and forward like serving motion, but gentle and controlled.
**Forehand swings:** Swing arm across body from low to high, like forehand follow-through.

#### Shoulder Cross-body & External Rotation
Combine the cross-body shoulder stretch with external rotation movement. Hold cross-body stretch, then rotate arm outward gently. Prepares shoulders for serving and overhead shots.

#### Hip Circles & Leg Swings
**Hip circles:** Stand on one leg, make circles with other leg (front, side, back).
**Leg swings:** Hold wall, swing leg forward/back and side to side. Prepares hips for court movement and lunging.

#### Gentle Spinal Rotation
Stand with arms crossed or hands on hips. Rotate torso left and right, increasing range gradually. Essential for tennis serve and groundstroke power. Keep hips facing forward.

#### Calf Raises & Ankle Mobility
**Calf raises:** Rise up on toes, lower slowly. Prepares calves for jumping and quick movements.
**Ankle circles:** Lift one foot, make circles with ankle. Prepares for lateral court movement and direction changes.

#### Shadow Tennis Movements
Perform slow, controlled tennis movements without ball:
- **Shadow serve:** Full serving motion at 50% speed
- **Shadow groundstrokes:** Forehand and backhand swings
- **Split steps:** Small jumps landing in ready position
- **Side shuffles:** Lateral movement like covering the court
