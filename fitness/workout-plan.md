## Weekly Schedule {#weekly-schedule}

- **Monday**: Upper Body Strength + Metabolic Finisher (45 min)
- **Tuesday**: HIIT Cardio + Core + Flexibility (30 min) 
- **Wednesday**: Lower Body Power + Strength (45 min)
- **Thursday**: Pre-Tennis Prep + Tennis (15 min prep)
- **Friday**: Push/Pull Supersets + Sports Power (45 min)
- **Saturday**: Full Body Circuit + Conditioning (40 min)
- **Sunday**: Active Recovery + Deep Flexibility (20 min)

::: warning Fat Loss Focus
**Training frequency increased to 6 days** for accelerated fat loss while building muscle. Higher volume supports your 2,100 calorie deficit and 13% body fat reduction goal.
:::

## Strength Training Days {#strength-training}

::: danger Intensity Guidelines
**RPE Target: 7-8/10** - You should have 2-3 reps left in the tank. Focus on progressive overload while maintaining perfect form for injury prevention.
:::

### Monday - Upper Body Strength + Metabolic Finisher {#monday-upper-strength}

::: details Session Overview - 45 minutes total
**Focus:** Upper body muscle building + fat-burning finisher
**Equipment:** Compound movements + cables  
**Benefits:** Muscle growth, strength, and metabolic boost
:::

**Dynamic Warm-up (8 min)**
- Arm circles and shoulder rolls (2 min)
- Band pull-aparts (1 min) 
- Cat-cow + thoracic rotations (2 min)
- Light rowing or arm bike (3 min)

**Strength Training (28 min)**
1. **[Chest Press Machine](#chest-press-machine)** - 4 sets x 6-8 reps (*Start: 35-45kg*)
   - *Rest: 90 seconds between sets*
2. **[Lat Pulldown Wide Grip](#lat-pulldown-machine)** - 4 sets x 8-10 reps (*Start: 40-50kg*)
   - *Rest: 90 seconds between sets*
3. **Superset A (3 rounds, 60s rest):**
   - **[Shoulder Press Machine](#shoulder-press-machine)** - 10-12 reps (*Start: 20-30kg*)
   - **[Seated Cable Row](#seated-cable-row)** - 12-15 reps (*Start: 35-45kg*)
4. **Superset B (3 rounds, 45s rest):**
   - **[Cable Face Pulls](#cable-face-pulls)** - 15-20 reps (*Start: 15-25kg*)
   - **[Cable Tricep Extension](#cable-tricep-extension)** - 12-15 reps (*Start: 20-30kg*)

**Metabolic Finisher (7 min)**
**Upper Body Burnout Circuit** - 3 rounds, no rest between exercises:
- Push-ups (max reps in 45 seconds)
- Mountain climbers (45 seconds)
- Plank hold (30 seconds)  
- Rest 60 seconds between rounds

**Cool Down (2 min)**
- [Doorway chest stretch](#doorway-chest-stretch) - 30 seconds each arm
- [Cross-body shoulder stretch](#cross-body-shoulder-stretch) - 30 seconds each arm

### Wednesday - Lower Body Power + Strength {#wednesday-lower-power}

::: details Session Overview - 45 minutes total
**Focus:** Lower body power + strength for climbing and tennis
**Equipment:** Compound movements + plyometrics
**Benefits:** Explosive power, leg strength, athletic performance
:::

**Dynamic Warm-up (8 min)**
- Light walking/jogging (3 min)
- Leg swings + hip circles (2 min)
- Walking lunges + butt kicks (2 min)
- Ankle mobility (1 min)

**Power Development (12 min)**
1. **Jump Squats** - 4 sets x 6-8 explosive reps
   - *Rest: 90 seconds between sets*
2. **Lateral Bounds** - 3 sets x 8 each direction  
   - *Rest: 60 seconds between sets*

**Strength Training (20 min)**
1. **[Leg Press Machine](#leg-press-machine)** - 4 sets x 8-10 reps (*Start: 80-100kg*)
   - *Rest: 90 seconds between sets*
2. **Superset A (3 rounds, 60s rest):**
   - **[Leg Curl Machine](#leg-curl-machine)** - 10-12 reps (*Start: 20-30kg*)
   - **[Leg Extension Machine](#leg-extension-machine)** - 12-15 reps (*Start: 25-35kg*)
3. **Superset B (3 rounds, 45s rest):**
   - **[Seated Calf Raise](#seated-calf-raise-machine)** - 15-20 reps (*Start: 25-35kg*)
   - **Walking Lunges** - 20 total steps (bodyweight)

**Flexibility (5 min)**
- [Standing quad stretch](#standing-quad-stretch) - 45 seconds each leg
- [Hip flexor stretch](#hip-flexor-stretch-kneeling-lunge) - 45 seconds each side
- [Standing hamstring stretch](#standing-hamstring-stretch) - 45 seconds each leg

### Friday - Push/Pull Supersets + Sports Power {#friday-push-pull}

::: details Session Overview - 45 minutes total
**Focus:** Upper body hypertrophy + rotational power
**Equipment:** Cable machines + functional movements
**Benefits:** Muscle building, tennis serve power, golf swing strength
:::

**Dynamic Warm-up (6 min)**
- Arm circles + shoulder rolls (2 min)
- Band pull-aparts + external rotations (2 min)
- Torso twists + arm swings (2 min)

**Push/Pull Supersets (25 min)**
**Superset 1 (4 rounds, 90s rest):**
- **[Chest Press Machine](#chest-press-machine)** - 8-10 reps (*Progressive load*)
- **[Cable Row Wide Grip](#seated-cable-row)** - 10-12 reps (*Progressive load*)

**Superset 2 (4 rounds, 75s rest):**
- **[Shoulder Press Machine](#shoulder-press-machine)** - 10-12 reps
- **[Lat Pulldown Close Grip](#lat-pulldown-machine)** - 12-15 reps

**Superset 3 (3 rounds, 60s rest):**
- **[Tricep Dip Machine](#tricep-dip-machine)** - 12-15 reps
- **[Cable Face Pulls](#cable-face-pulls)** - 15-20 reps

**Sports Power Block (10 min)**
1. **[Cable Wood Chops](#cable-wood-chops)** - 3 sets x 8 each side (*explosive*)
2. **Medicine Ball Slams** - 3 sets x 10 (*if available, or explosive push-ups*)
3. **[Rotary Torso Machine](#rotary-torso-machine)** - 2 sets x 12 each direction

**Cool Down (4 min)**
- Chest + shoulder stretches (2 min)
- Spinal rotation stretch (2 min)

### Tuesday - HIIT Cardio + Core {#tuesday-hiit}

::: details Session Overview - 30 minutes total
**Focus:** Metabolic conditioning + core strength
**Equipment:** Cardio machines + bodyweight
**Benefits:** Fat burning, tennis endurance, core stability
:::

**Warm-up (5 min)**
- Light cardio (treadmill, bike, or rower) - build to moderate intensity

**HIIT Cardio Block (15 min)**
**Option 1 - Treadmill Intervals:**
- 3 min moderate walk (3-4% incline)
- 8 rounds: 30s sprint (6-8% incline) + 90s recovery walk
- 2 min cool down walk

**Option 2 - Bike/Rower Intervals:**
- 3 min moderate pace
- 8 rounds: 30s all-out + 90s easy pace  
- 2 min cool down

**Core Strength Circuit (8 min)**
**3 rounds, 45s work / 15s rest:**
- Dead bugs (alternating)
- Plank to downward dog
- Russian twists
- Bicycle crunches

**Flexibility (2 min)**
- Hip flexor stretch - 30s each side
- Spinal twist - 30s each side

### Saturday - Full Body Circuit + Conditioning {#saturday-circuit}

::: details Session Overview - 40 minutes total
**Focus:** Full body conditioning + metabolic challenge
**Equipment:** Circuit training + bodyweight
**Benefits:** Fat burning, muscular endurance, functional fitness
:::

**Dynamic Warm-up (6 min)**
- Joint mobility sequence (2 min)
- Bodyweight movement prep (2 min)
- Light cardio buildup (2 min)

**Full Body Strength Circuit (20 min)**
**Circuit A - 4 rounds, 45s work/15s rest, 90s rest between rounds:**
1. **[Chest Press Machine](#chest-press-machine)** - moderate weight, 12-15 reps
2. **[Leg Press Machine](#leg-press-machine)** - moderate weight, 15-20 reps
3. **Bodyweight Squats** - maximum reps
4. **[Seated Cable Row](#seated-cable-row)** - 12-15 reps
5. **Push-ups** - maximum reps (modify as needed)

**Metabolic Finisher (10 min)**
**EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute) - 10 rounds:**
- Minute 1: 15 Mountain climbers + 10 Jump squats
- Minute 2: 10 Burpees (or modified burpees)
- *Use remaining time in each minute to rest*

**Cool Down (4 min)**
- Full body flow stretches
- Deep breathing and relaxation

## Progression Guidelines {#progression}

### Fat Loss & Body Recomposition Focus

**Weeks 1-2: Foundation Building**
- Focus on form and movement patterns
- RPE 6-7/10 (moderate intensity)
- Complete all prescribed sets and reps
- Track body weight and measurements

**Weeks 3-4: Intensity Increase**
- Increase weights by 10-15% where possible
- RPE 7-8/10 (challenging but controlled)
- Add 5-10% more cardio volume if energy allows
- Monitor strength maintenance while losing fat

**Weeks 5-8: Progressive Overload**
- Continue weight increases every 2 weeks
- Add extra sets to lagging muscle groups
- Increase metabolic finisher difficulty
- Assess if additional cardio is needed

**Weeks 9-16: Final Push**
- Focus on strength maintenance during deeper deficit
- Increase conditioning frequency if fat loss stalls
- Prioritize recovery and flexibility work
- Prepare for transition to maintenance/building phase

### Weight Progression Guidelines

**Upper Body Machines:**
- Week 1-2: Start weights as listed
- Week 3-4: Increase by 10-15%
- Target progression: 2.5-5kg increases every 2-3 weeks

**Lower Body Machines:**
- Week 1-2: Start weights as listed  
- Week 3-4: Increase by 15-20%
- Target progression: 5-10kg increases every 2-3 weeks

### Cardio Progression

**HIIT Sessions:**
- Week 1-2: Complete as prescribed
- Week 3-4: Increase sprint intervals by 15 seconds
- Week 5+: Add extra round or increase incline/resistance

## Health & Safety {#health-safety}

::: danger Important Safety Guidelines
Read this section carefully before starting this intensive routine.
:::

### Managing Higher Training Volume

**Pre-Workout Preparation:**
- Eat 200-300 calories 1-2 hours before training
- Consume 500-600ml water 30-60 minutes before
- Ensure adequate sleep (7-9 hours) for recovery

**During Workouts:**
- Sip water between exercises, don't chug
- Take prescribed rest periods seriously  
- Monitor heart rate during HIIT (stay below 85% max)
- Stop immediately if experiencing dizziness or nausea

**Recovery Between Sessions:**
- Prioritize protein intake within 2 hours post-workout
- Aim for 140g protein daily to preserve muscle
- Get quality sleep - crucial during deficit
- Listen to your body - take extra rest days if needed

**Red Flags - Reduce Volume or Take Rest Day:**
- Persistent fatigue lasting >2 days
- Declining strength for 2+ consecutive sessions
- Sleep disruption or increased stress
- Joint pain or unusual muscle soreness
- Extreme hunger or food preoccupation
