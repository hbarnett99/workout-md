# Baseline Strength Assessment

::: info Purpose
Establish accurate starting weights for your workout plan based on your actual strength levels. Your Evolt scan shows optimal muscle mass (32.6kg) suggesting you may be stronger than beginner assumptions.
:::

## Assessment Protocol

### Pre-Assessment Preparation
- **Timing:** Complete when well-rested, not after a workout
- **Warm-up:** 5-10 minutes light cardio + dynamic stretching
- **Ankle focus:** Extra ankle mobility given your injury history
- **Hydration:** Well-hydrated but not immediately post-meal

### Testing Method
- **1 set to near failure** for each exercise (leave 1-2 reps in reserve)
- **Rest 2-3 minutes** between exercises
- **Focus on form** over maximum weight
- **Record weight and reps** achieved

## Upper Body Assessment

### Chest Press Machine
**Setup:** Seat height so handles align with mid-chest
**Starting weight:** 20-25kg
**Target:** 8-15 reps to near failure
**Form focus:** Controlled movement, don't lock elbows

### Lat Pulldown
**Setup:** Wide grip, slight lean back
**Starting weight:** 30-35kg  
**Target:** 8-15 reps to near failure
**Form focus:** Pull to upper chest, squeeze shoulder blades

### Shoulder Press Machine
**Setup:** Handles at shoulder height when seated
**Starting weight:** 15-20kg
**Target:** 8-15 reps to near failure
**Form focus:** Press up and slightly forward

### Seated Cable Row
**Setup:** Neutral grip, feet on platform
**Starting weight:** 25-30kg
**Target:** 8-15 reps to near failure
**Form focus:** Pull to lower ribs, maintain posture

## Lower Body Assessment

### [Leg Press Machine](exercise-guides.md#leg-press-machine)
**Starting weight:** 40-60kg (including machine weight)
**Target:** 10-20 reps to near failure

### [Leg Curl Machine](exercise-guides.md#leg-curl-machine)
**Starting weight:** 15-25kg
**Target:** 10-15 reps to near failure

### [Hip Abductor Machine](exercise-guides.md#hip-abductor-machine)
**Starting weight:** 20-30kg
**Target:** 12-20 reps to near failure

### [Seated Calf Raise](exercise-guides.md#seated-calf-raise-machine)
**Starting weight:** 20-30kg
**Target:** 15-25 reps to near failure

## Functional Assessment

### Plank Hold
**Position:** Forearms and toes, straight body line
**Target:** Hold to failure (aim for 30+ seconds)
**Form focus:** No sagging hips, normal breathing

### Push-ups
**Position:** Standard or modified (knees down)
**Target:** Maximum reps to failure
**Form focus:** Full range of motion, straight body

### Single-Leg Balance (Ankle Assessment)
**Position:** One foot, eyes open, arms for balance
**Target:** Time to failure each leg
**Progression:** Start with wall support if needed
**Critical:** Stop if any ankle discomfort

## Recording Your Results

### Assessment Sheet
Create a simple log with:
- **Exercise name**
- **Weight used (kg)**
- **Reps completed**
- **RPE (1-10 difficulty)**
- **Notes** (form, comfort, ankle status)

### Example Entry
```
Chest Press Machine: 25kg x 12 reps, RPE 8
Notes: Form good, could have done 2 more reps
```

## Post-Assessment Analysis

### Strength Categories
Based on your results, we'll categorize as:
- **Beginner:** 8-12 reps at starting weights
- **Intermediate:** 12+ reps at starting weights  
- **Advanced:** 15+ reps, need higher starting weights

### Ankle Evaluation
- **Balance time achieved** on each leg
- **Any discomfort** during movements
- **Compensation patterns** observed
- **Confidence level** for tennis activities

## Using Results for Your Program

### Starting Weight Calculation
**Week 1-2 workout weights:**
- Use 70-80% of your assessment weight
- Focus on form and movement patterns
- Build confidence with ankle-safe exercises

**Week 3+ progression:**
- Increase based on assessment performance
- Higher assessment = faster progression
- Always prioritize ankle safety

### Program Modifications
**If stronger than expected:**
- Start with higher calorie target (2400-2500)
- More aggressive progression schedule
- Additional exercises if recovery allows

**If ankle limitations found:**
- Extended ankle rehabilitation phase
- Modified exercise selection
- Slower return to tennis activities

## Recovery Protocol

### Immediately Post-Assessment
- Light 5-10 minute walk
- Gentle stretching, focus on areas tested
- **Ankle-specific:** Alphabet, gentle circles
- Hydrate well

### 24-48 Hours After
- Monitor for excessive soreness
- Note any ankle discomfort
- Light movement/walking as able
- Plan first official workout 2-3 days later

### Red Flags
**Stop and reassess if:**
- Significant ankle pain/swelling
- Extreme muscle soreness lasting 3+ days
- Any exercise caused sharp pain
- Balance issues worsened
